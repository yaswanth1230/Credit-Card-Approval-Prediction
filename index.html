About This Project

CreditAI is a Machine Learning based Credit Card Approval Prediction System developed to estimate whether an applicant is likely to receive credit card approval.

The prediction considers multiple applicant details such as annual income, employment status, education, family details, loan history, EMI records and property ownership.
